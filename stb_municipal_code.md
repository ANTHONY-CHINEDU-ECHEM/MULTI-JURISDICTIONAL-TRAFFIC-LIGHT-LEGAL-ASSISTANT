# Stonebridge Municipal Code Title 10 Signals

Jurisdiction: City of Stonebridge, State of Kestrel (tier: LOCAL)

Notice: SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. This jurisdiction and its provisions are fictional and exist only to exercise the multi jurisdictional reasoning of this software. It is not the law of any real state or municipality.

## Section 1.01 Adoption of State Policy

Standard:
01
The City of Stonebridge adopts the Kestrel DOT Signal Operations Directive for all traffic control signals under municipal jurisdiction. Where this ordinance is more restrictive, this ordinance governs.

## Section 1.02 Minimum Yellow Change Interval

Standard:
01
No yellow change interval at a municipal signal shall be less than 3.5 seconds, regardless of the value produced by the state equation.

## Section 1.03 Minimum Red Clearance Interval

Standard:
01
Every signal phase shall display a red clearance interval of not less than 1.2 seconds before a conflicting movement is released.

## Section 1.04 High Speed Approaches

Standard:
01
On approaches with a posted speed limit of 50 mph or greater the yellow change interval shall be not less than 4.5 seconds.

## Section 1.06 Detector Repair

Standard:
01
A vehicle detector failure at a municipal signal shall be repaired within 24 hours of detection by controller diagnostics or report.

Support:
02
Failure to repair within this period is evidence that the municipality had notice of a defective condition.

## Section 1.07 Signal Records

Standard:
01
Controller event logs, detector fault logs and malfunction management unit logs shall be retained for not less than 540 days and shall be preserved without alteration after notice of a crash or claim.
